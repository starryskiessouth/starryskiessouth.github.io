About Us

Mission & Scope

Our mission is to promote appreciation and conservation of starry skies across the southeastern United States.

Our Region

Our region includes Alabama, Georgia, Mississippi, North Carolina*, and South Carolina. We welcome participants from other places across the southeastern United States, per their interest. To participate, membership to DarkSky International is recommended but not required. *﻿North Carolina also has a state-based chapter, DarkSky North Carolina. Some members in DarkSky NC also participate in Starry Skies South.
